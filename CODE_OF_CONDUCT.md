# Code of Conduct

The Solum Triality project is maintained primarily by a single individual as a hobby, but it welcomes contributions from everyone. Our community is built on mutual respect and a shared desire to learn and create. To keep our spaces welcoming and productive, we ask all participants to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participant behavior, and explains how to report unacceptable behavior. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant version 2.1 and other community resources.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in communication and actions.
- Assume good faith and focus on constructive collaboration.
- Listen when others raise concerns and take them seriously.
- Give and receive feedback graciously and responsibly.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.

Impact matters more than intent: even unintentional harm should be acknowledged and addressed. If someone tells you that your behavior caused harm, listen, apologize if appropriate, and take steps to make it right.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable and may lead to removal from project spaces or other enforcement actions:

- Harassment, threats, or abusive language.
- Persistent unwanted attention after a clear request to stop (including "sea-lioning").
- Insults, character attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Stereotyping, discrimination, or exclusion based on identity or background.
- Sexualized behavior, unwelcome sexual attention, or sexual content in inappropriate contexts.
- Sharing someone's private or confidential information without permission (doxxing).
- Encouraging, threatening, or glorifying violence or other harm.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or participating in an investigation.
- Repeated bad-faith reporting intended to harass or burden maintainers or volunteers.

This list is not exhaustive. Behaviors that undermine the well-being of the community are also covered.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Solum Triality project or Solum Solus, including the issue tracker, pull requests, mailing lists, chat channels, events, and any other project-organized forums.

## Reporting

If you believe someone is violating this Code of Conduct, please report it by emailing: haydaust@gmail.com

When reporting, include as much relevant information as you can: what happened, when and where it happened (links if applicable), who was involved, and any supporting evidence. Reporters may request confidentiality; we will do our best to respect it while fulfilling our responsibility to the community.

## Enforcement

Reports will be handled by the project maintainers and, when appropriate, short-term or ad-hoc conduct stewards. After a report is received we will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report as soon as practical.
2. Assess the situation and, if needed, gather more information from the parties involved.
3. Determine appropriate actions, which may include warnings, temporary or permanent removal from project spaces, or other remedies.

Enforcement decisions aim to be fair and proportional to the behavior and its impact. We may escalate serious incidents to broader maintainers or host organizations if appropriate.
